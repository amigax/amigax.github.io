FEROX WIRE

A vector VJ screen. Give it music and it does the rest — autopilot is already on.

1 · GET THE MUSIC IN

Any one of these three:

  • Drag an audio file onto this page. It plays on a loop.
  • Press L to use the sound of another tab or of the whole computer. Pick the source in the box the browser opens and tick “Also share tab audio” (or “share system audio”). Good for YouTube, Spotify, a DJ set, a DAW. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. Chrome or Edge. On a Mac only tab audio works.
  • Press M for a mic, line in, mixer or audio interface, then SHIFT+M to step through the inputs until the name you want flashes up.

Playing live through Ableton on an ASIO driver? L will not hear it. Use your interface's loopback, or a virtual cable, and pick it with SHIFT+M.

2 · WATCH IT

  • It picks the effects and finds the tempo by itself. Press . if you would rather choose.
  • Double-click for full screen. TAB hides these readouts.
  • / types your own words into the visuals — a name, a venue, a track title.

3 · PLAY WITH IT

1 – 0pick an effect · ` for the next bank of ten
SHIFT + 1-0cut to it instantly instead of a fade
.autopilot: full / effects only / off
SPACEtap the tempo · SHIFT+SPACE back to automatic
Z Xmorph or mix between effects · fade length
Ccolour · SHIFT+C kaleidoscope

; ’ - =fold and crush the picture
ESC ESCback to normal

NOTHING IS MOVING?

  • Check the top left: it shows the tempo and whether it is hearing anything.
  • With L, the audio tick box is easy to miss — press L again and make sure it is ticked.
  • With M, tap SHIFT+M a few times; the first input is often the wrong one.
